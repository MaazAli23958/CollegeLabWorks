2. Copy, Move, and Remove Files (cp, mv, rm)
These are essential commands for managing files.

•	cp (copy): Creates a duplicate of a file.
o	Example: cp copy.txt copy_backup.txt

•	mv (move): Moves or renames a file.
o	Rename Example: mv rename.txt new_rename.txt
o	Move Example: mv move.txt ./moved/

•	rm (remove): Deletes files. This is permanent!
o	Example: rm delete.txt
o	To remove a directory and its contents, use the recursive -r flag: rm -r old_project/
